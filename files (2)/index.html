Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about document requests, queueing, and Registrar's Office hours. Still have a question? Send us feedback below.

How do I request a document online?

Choose “Request a Document” or “Book a Slot” to sign in and start a request. You can also visit the on-site kiosk for walk-in service.

Can I walk in without booking online?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome through the kiosk, and kiosk visitors join the same live queue as online bookings.

Which documents can I request?

Available requests include a Certificate of Registration, Certificate of Grades, Transcript of Records, and Certificate of Enrollment.

How can I check the queue or estimated wait?

Sign in to your UniQueue account to view the live queue and estimated wait time for your request.

When is the Registrar's Office open?

The office is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, at the Main Building.
